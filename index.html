What we do

Exterior services for Connecticut homes

From a single repair to a full outside refresh, every job is scoped and priced before we start.

Siding & Additions

Vinyl, cedar and fiber-cement siding, plus repairs, replacement and trim work that matches existing materials.

Roofing

Asphalt shingle roofs — tear-offs, re-roofs, repairs, flashing and gutters — built to stand up to New England weather.

Decks & Porches

Custom decks, porches, awnings and railings in composite or wood — from a sketch to a finished outdoor space.

Windows & Doors

Window replacement, entry and patio doors, storm doors and trim — tighter, quieter and more efficient.

Gutters & Exterior Trim

Seamless gutters, fascia, soffit and leader replacement that keeps water away from your foundation.

Painting & Repairs

Exterior painting, rotten-wood repairs and millwork replacement to bring tired siding back to life.

Who we are
