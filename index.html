About Juanes Home Improvement

The Family-Owned Home Improvement Company Committed to Craftsmanship

Since 2013, Juanes Home Improvement has been a go-to choice for homeowners seeking exterior remodeling in New Haven County, Middlesex County and the Connecticut Shoreline. As a family-owned business, we bring our commitment to quality and craftsmanship to every project - roofing, windows, doors and gutters that improve your home's look and hold up over time. Our bilingual team keeps communication clear from the first estimate through the final walkthrough, and many jobs move fast: roofing installations are often completed in one to two days. Every project is backed by a two-year workmanship warranty.
